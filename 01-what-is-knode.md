# 1 · What Knode is

Knode turns your notes and your AI conversations into a **map of knowledge**: every idea is a
node, and nodes link to each other. The map is **yours**, saved in an open `.knode` file you can
read, move and keep without Knode.

## Principles

These decide the hard calls. When two options look equal, pick the one that respects them best.

1. **Yours.** Your thoughts live in files you own, on your devices. No lock-in, no "our data".
2. **Open.** The `.knode` format is documented and stable; the code is open source.
3. **Native.** Built for each platform with its own tools, starting with macOS and iOS.
4. **Local first.** Everything works offline; anything that leaves your device is your choice.
5. **Calm.** No noise, no streaks, no magic tricks. Knode helps you think; it doesn't perform.
6. **Simple, then deep.** Easy the first minute, powerful after a year.

## Who it's for

People who think a lot (and talk to AI a lot) and want what they've thought to add up to
something they can see, explore and keep.
